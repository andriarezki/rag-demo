# New Employee Onboarding (English)

## First week

On the first day you receive your laptop, a building access card and your company email. HR runs a welcome session at 10:00 and the IT team runs a short security briefing at 14:00. You are expected to complete the security awareness course within your first 14 days.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 on weekdays. You may work from home up to 2 days per week after your probation period of 3 months. During probation, please work from the office unless your manager agrees otherwise.

## Who to ask

For IT problems write in the #bantuan-it channel. For leave, payslips and contracts ask HR. For expenses and reimbursements ask the finance team.
